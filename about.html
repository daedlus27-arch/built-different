Why this blog?

Because my life keeps happening at me.

I moved to Los Santos for a fresh start. New city, new job, new everything. Within a few weeks I’d been jumped, hit by a car, hired by the government and somehow talked a girl into exclusivity, so I figured somebody should be writing this stuff down.

This is that somebody. It’s me. I’m the somebody.

Expect honest updates, bad decisions, the occasional tech tip, and absolutely no proofreading after midnight. Don’t expect corrections. See the contact page for why.
